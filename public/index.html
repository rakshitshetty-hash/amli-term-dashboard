How these numbers are built
  • Leads come from the unique lead dump, dated by lead creation date. Sales and APE come from the Direct & D2C sales dump, dated by payment date. Spend comes from the Daily Spends Tracker (Term All Channels).
  • Line items follow the Master Tracker mapping. NewBlank7 sits in Brand. Bottom Funnel = Brand + Core + Others. Display = PR + RM. On this tab, Netcore includes the cross-sell and upsell journeys, as in the plan; the Day on day tab shows the Netcore line item alone, against its own plan rows.
  • Targets are the Nov'26 media plan. Month-to-date and daily targets follow the plan's daily phasing (QL curve for leads, NOP and APE curves for sales and APE), not a straight split by days.
  • Total sales and APE targets use the plan's unique-sale figures (8,688 sales, ₹25.49 Cr APE). Group targets add up to more because some sales count in more than one group.
  • CVR divides sales by payment date by leads by creation date, so the latest days read low until those leads pay.
  • "Furthest behind" looks only at groups with a month APE target of ₹100 L or more.
